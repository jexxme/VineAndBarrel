Some art, the boiling sounds, and the Brew Kettle, rope-climbing, wild-crop and biome-filter code are adapted from Farmer's Delight by vectorwing (MIT).

They come from Farmer's Delight (https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight), via Farmer's Delight Neoforged, used
under the MIT License below. FD's file names, then ours.

Art:
- heating_tray_top.png, heating_tray_side.png (as block/brew_kettle_tray_top.png, block/brew_kettle_tray_side.png):
  the Brew Kettle's tray, exactly like FD's cooking pot tray.
- cooking_pot_gui.png (as gui/brew_kettle.png): base of the Brew Kettle screen (FD's layout, slots, flames, arrow, ladle);
  only the pot shape is replaced by our kettle.
- cooking_pot_handle.png (as block/brew_kettle_handle.png): the Brew Kettle's hanging handle, exactly like FD's cooking
  pot handle.
- rope_top.png (as block/rope_top.png) and the rope fibres in block/grape_vine_coiled_rope.png and
  block/hop_vine_coiled_rope.png: FD's rope, so a vine on a rope looks like it grows on FD's rope;
  models/block/template_crop_with_rope.json is FD's model; models/block/template_vine_wound.json is its crop planes
  without the rope (a vine wound round a chain or a post).
- recipe_book/cooking_pot_*.png (as gui/sprites/recipe_book/brew_kettle_*.png): base of the Brew Kettle's recipe book
  button; only the pot icon is replaced by our kettle.
- cooking_pot/boil_water1-2.ogg, boil_soup1-3.ogg (as sounds/block/brew_kettle/boil_water1-2.ogg, boil_brew1-3.ogg): the Brew Kettle's boiling sounds.
- The plate and bowl under the meal icons (beer_battered_fish, wine_braised_beef, cider_braised_pork,
  white_wine_risotto, barley_mutton_broth, coq_au_vin): FD's plate and bowl pixels; the food on them is our own.

Code (adapted):
- block/WildCropBlock: FD's WildCropBlock.
- block/VineOnRopeBlock and block/VineClimbing (its rope part): FD's TomatoBlock rope climbing and HangingTomatoBlock rope permanence.
- neoforge/AddFeaturesByFilterBiomeModifier (NeoForge only): FD's add_features_by_filter biome modifier.
- mixin/CampfireSignalSmokeMixin: the approach of FD's CampfireBaleMixin.
- kettle/*: The Brew Kettle's block, block entity, menu, screen, recipe, recipe book, heat rules, hopper access and item tooltip follow FD's cooking pot.
- compat/jei/BrewingCategory: FD's JEI cooking category.
- compat/farmersdelight/FeastBlock: the Coq au Vin feast follows FD's FeastBlock (servings taken with a bowl,
  leftovers, comparator output).
- world/village/VillageStructures#addBuildingToPool: FD's VillageStructures#addBuildingToPool.
