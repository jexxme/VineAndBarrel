Some art and code in this mod come from Farmer's Delight (https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight),
via Farmer's Delight Neoforged, used under the MIT License below.

Art:
- heating_tray_top.png, heating_tray_side.png: the Brew Kettle's tray, exactly like FD's cooking pot tray.
- cooking_pot_gui.png: base of the Brew Kettle screen (FD's layout, slots, flames, arrow, ladle); only the pot shape is replaced by our kettle.
- cooking_pot_handle.png: the Brew Kettle's hanging handle, exactly like FD's cooking pot handle.
- rope_top.png and the rope fibres in grape_vine_coiled_rope.png / hop_vine_coiled_rope.png: FD's rope, so a vine on
  a rope looks like it grows on FD's rope; models/block/template_crop_with_rope.json is FD's model.
- recipe_book/cooking_pot_*.png: base of the Brew Kettle's recipe book button; only the pot icon is replaced by our kettle.
- steam_0.png (as particle/steam.png): the Brew Kettle's steam, exactly like FD's cooking pot steam.
- cooking_pot/boil_water1-2.ogg, boil_soup1-3.ogg (as brew_kettle/boil_water1-2.ogg, boil_brew1-3.ogg): the Brew Kettle's boiling sounds.

Code (adapted):
- block/WildCropBlock: FD's WildCropBlock.
- block/VineOnRopeBlock and block/RopeClimbing: FD's TomatoBlock rope climbing and HangingTomatoBlock rope permanence.
- world/AddFeaturesByFilterBiomeModifier: FD's add_features_by_filter biome modifier.
- mixin/CampfireSignalSmokeMixin: the approach of FD's CampfireBaleMixin.
- kettle/*: The Brew Kettle's block, block entity, menu, screen, recipe, recipe book, heat rules, hopper access, steam particle and item tooltip follow FD's cooking pot.
