<Aside type="caution" title="Spoilers">
This page tells you every way a night can end. If you'd rather find out for yourself, stop here: the
[Tipsy](/tipsy/#one-drink-too-many) page only hints at it.
</Aside>

Already at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)} and still drinking? One more and you **black out**. It's harmless: nothing you had
is ever lost, you never wake up anywhere unsafe, no time passes, and nobody else is affected. Server owners can switch it
off with the `blackout` option (see [Configuration](/reference/config/)).

## What happens

<Steps>

1. **You keel over.** Your eyes fall shut and you tip over. Everyone around you sees you lying there, stars circling
   your head.
2. **Black.** For a few seconds you can't move, and Tipsy lets you be: no sway, no hiccups, no swirl. Nobody can hurt
   you either, no mob and no player, and falling over doesn't. Only what would hurt anyone lying there still counts,
   like lava or the void, so where you black out matters; where you *wake up* is always safe.
3. **You wake up somewhere.** While the screen is still black, the game picks where your night ended (see below) and
   puts you there: lying on the ground, in a bed, or sitting in a boat. Your eyes open slowly, then you get up.
4. **A line on the screen** says how it went, *{b.endings.find((e) => e.id === 'in_the_hay')?.lines[0]}* or one of many others.

</Steps>

The whole thing takes about {seconds(b.ticks)}. You wake up:

- **back at Tipsy I**, and without the buffs your drinks gave you,
- **hungry:** {b.hungerLost / 2} hunger shanks lighter, and nothing in your saturation,
- **shaky** for {seconds(b.shakyTicks)}: a little dizzy and slow,
- **with a souvenir** in your pockets, something that fits where you woke up (at your feet if your pockets are full),
- and, one time in {b.pumpkinOneIn}, **with a carved pumpkin on your head**, if you weren't wearing a helmet.

## Where you wake up

There are {count} places a night can end, plus one for when none of them works out. Each has its own chance, but only the
ones that are possible where you blacked out are in the draw: no water nearby, no boat; no village, no tavern.

- **New ones first:** an ending you haven't had yet is {b.unseenBonus} times as likely as one you have.
- **Never twice in a row,** unless nothing else is possible.
- **Always safe:** solid ground, room to stand, no lava or water, nothing that burns or pricks next to you, no monster
  within {b.ranges.monsterFree} blocks, inside the world border.
- **Never inside someone's build:** the outdoor endings need open sky above, glass and leaves included. The two indoor
  ones only use what the world built for everyone, a village tavern and a villager's bed in a village house, and your own bed.
- **In the Nether and the End** there's no open sky, so most nights end right where you fell.

The odds below are for your first blackout with every ending possible. After that they shift: the endings you haven't
had yet get more likely, and the one you just had steps aside.

## Collect them all

The first blackout earns *Where Am I?*. Wake up in all {count} places (*Right here* doesn't count) for the challenge
*Lost Weekend*. The game remembers which ones you've had, even through death.

<Aside type="tip" title="Hunting for a particular ending?">
Set the stage before the last drink: a bed of your own nearby for *Home*, a hay or <Item id="barley_bale" /> stack outside
for *In the hay*, a pond for *In a boat*, a village with a tavern for the indoor ones. Endings you've already had are less
likely, so the rare ones come up sooner than you'd think.
</Aside>
