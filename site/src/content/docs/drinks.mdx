Every drink follows the same rules:

- It gives **one buff** and one level of [Tipsy](/tipsy/). A drink that takes you to Tipsy {roman(rules.tipsy.noBuffLevel - 1)} or higher gives
  no buff at all: you're too drunk to enjoy it.
- You can always drink it, even when you're full. It takes {sample.consumable.seconds} s, and you get the empty
  <Item id="mug" /> or <Item id="wine_glass" /> back.
- It stacks to **{sample.stack}**.
- A **young** drink's buff is level {roman(rules.youngBuffAmplifier)} for **{duration(rules.youngBuffTicks)}**. The **aged** version has a deeper
  colour, shimmers like an enchanted item, and its buff is level **{roman(rules.agedBuffAmplifier)} for {duration(rules.agedBuffTicks)}**. Wheat beer's
  Saturation works at once instead: a few food points.
- Put down on a table, an aged drink shimmers like an enchanted item, so you can tell it from a young one at a glance.

## Colours

Every drink has its own colour: you see it in the barrel's sight glass and in the mugs and glasses on a table. Aged
drinks are a shade deeper.

Saturation works differently from the other buffs: it doesn't last, each tick of it fills one hunger point (and some
saturation) right away. That's why wheat beer shows food points instead of a time.

## Vessels

<Render name="scenes/placed-drinks" float="right" size={192} alt="Mugs and glasses on a block" />

Wine goes in a <Item id="wine_glass" />; beer, cider and mead in a <Item id="mug" />. Both stack to {items['vineandbarrel:mug'].stack}.

**Set them down:** sneak and right-click the top of a block with a mug or glass, full or empty, to put it there. Up to four
share one block, each keeping its own drink, and you can see the drink through the glass. Right-click with an empty hand to pick one back up.

## Cheers!

When two or more players finish a drink within {rules.cheersWindowTicks / 20} seconds and {rules.cheersRadius} blocks of each
other, glasses clink, a few sparkles go up, and everyone's buff from that drink lasts **{rules.cheersBonusPercent}% longer**.

It only counts between players in the same world, once per drink, and your own two drinks don't make a round. The bonus is
{rules.cheersBonusPercent}% of that drink's full buff time, added to what's left; if the buff has just run out, you get it back for that long.

Drinking alone? Finish your drink in front of a [tavern keeper](/villages/#tavern-keeper) and he clinks with you: you get the
same bonus.

## Good to know

- **Too many?** The drink that takes you to Tipsy {roman(rules.tipsy.noBuffLevel - 1)} gives no buff, and it also takes away the
  buffs your earlier drinks gave you. Effects from potions stay.
- **Tables:** a drink goes on the quarter of the block you aim at, or the nearest free one, and only on a sturdy top.
  An empty-hand right-click picks up the one you aim at; holding something, you use that item as usual, so you can drink
  at the table. Breaking the block, a piston or losing the block underneath drops them all.
- **Drinks as ingredients** (in Farmer's Delight's cooking pot) give you the mug or glass back.
- **Full stomach?** It doesn't matter: you can always drink.
