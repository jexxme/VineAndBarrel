The <Item id="aging_barrel" /> is the heart of Vine & Barrel. It has no screen: you pour into it, wait, and tap from it, all in
the world. It's placed with the tap facing you.

## Filling

Right-click the barrel with a base from the [Brew Kettle](/brew-kettle/):

- a bucket pours in **{rules.servingsPerBucket} servings**, a bottle **{rules.servingsPerBottle}**,
- the barrel holds **{rules.barrelCapacity} servings**: two buckets, or eight bottles.

You can top it up with more of the same base while it's still fermenting. Poured the wrong thing? Until the batch has turned
into a drink, an empty bucket or bottle takes it back out.

## Fermenting and aging

{sameTimes && <p>Every batch goes through the same two stages:</p>}

1. **Fermenting**, for **{days(a.fermentingTime)}**. The batch becomes the *young* drink, such as <Item id="red_wine" />.
2. **Aging**, for **{days(a.agingTime)}** more. Whatever is still in the barrel becomes the *aged* drink, such as <Item id="aged_red_wine" />, whose buff is stronger and lasts longer.

The clock runs on in-game time, so sleeping through the night counts. You can tap young drinks during the aging stage, but
every serving you take is one less that ages. With Farmer's Delight, its apple cider and melon juice ferment too: see
[Farmer's Delight](/farmers-delight/).

## Watching it work

You can tell how a barrel is doing without opening anything:

- **Empty-hand right-click** shows a status line, e.g. *Barley Ale · 5 servings · aging, aged in 3 days*. Rename a barrel in
  an anvil and the name shows up there too.
- **The bung on top** shows the stage: a cork when it's empty or the drink is young and ready, a glass airlock that bubbles
  while it ferments, and a red wax seal once it's aged.
- **The sight glass** on the front shows how full it is, in the colour of the drink.
- **Sounds and drips:** bubbles while it ferments, the tap drips now and then, and a soft cork *pop* when the batch turns aged.

## Tapping

Right-click the barrel with an empty <Item id="wine_glass" /> (wines) or <Item id="mug" /> (beers and cider) to tap one serving.
The tap handle turns and the drink lands in your hand.

- Wrong vessel? The barrel tells you which one to use (*Red Wine is served in a Wine Glass*), and nothing is used up.
- Still fermenting? It tells you when it's ready.
- Once the last serving is tapped, the barrel is empty and ready for a new batch.

## Building a cellar

<Render name="scenes/barrel-cellar" float="right" size={256} alt="Two rows of barrels, the top row resting on the bottom one" />

- **Racks:** barrels side by side, facing the same way, join their cradles into one long rack.
- **Stacks:** a barrel on top of another drops its cradle and rests on chocks, so you can build a whole wall of casks.
- **Comparator:** reads the servings left, from 0 (empty) to 15 (all {rules.barrelCapacity}).
- **Break it full** and it keeps its batch. The item's tooltip shows the drink, servings and stage. Aging pauses while it's
  an item and carries on when you place it again.
- Barrels are wooden: mine them with an axe.

## Automation

- **Hoppers** from above pour bases in. From the sides and below, they feed in empty mugs and glasses and pull out the
  tapped drinks, and the empty buckets and bottles left over from pouring.
- **Dispensers** facing a barrel tap a serving with an empty mug or glass, or pour in a bucket or bottle of base.
