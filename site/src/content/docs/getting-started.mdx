Vine & Barrel adds one loop: **grow → brew → age → serve**. You need two blocks for it, the
<Item id="brew_kettle" /> and the <Item id="aging_barrel" />, and something to drink from: a <Item id="wine_glass" /> or a <Item id="mug" />.

<Render name="scenes/garden" float="right" size={256} alt="Grape and hop bushes next to a barley field" />

<Steps>

1. **Find seeds.** Wild grapes, hops and barley grow out in the world: grapes in warm forests and plains, hops in forests and
   along rivers, barley on plains and savannas. Village farmers and the wandering trader sell them too, and village
   chests often hold a few. See [Crops](/crops/).

2. **Plant and harvest.** Plant <Item id="red_grapes" plural /> or <Item id="hops" /> like sweet berries and pick them with
   a right-click once ripe. Sow <Item id="barley_seeds" /> on farmland like wheat.

3. **Build a kettle and heat it.** Put the <Item id="brew_kettle" /> on a campfire, fire, lava, a magma block or a lava
   cauldron. With Farmer's Delight, its stove works too.

4. **Brew.** Put the ingredients in and wait {seconds(juice.brewingTime)}. Then fill a glass bottle (1 serving) or a bucket
   (all {juice.servings}). See [Brew Kettle](/brew-kettle/).

   <Recipe id="brewing/grape_juice" />

5. **Pour it into a barrel.** Right-click an <Item id="aging_barrel" /> with the bucket or bottles. It holds
   {rules.barrelCapacity} servings. After **{days(wine.fermentingTime)}** it has fermented into <Item id="red_wine" />, and after
   **{days(wine.agingTime)}** more, whatever is left becomes <Item id="aged_red_wine" />. See [Aging Barrel](/aging-barrel/).

6. **Tap and share.** Right-click the barrel with an empty <Item id="wine_glass" /> (wine) or <Item id="mug" /> (beer) to
   tap one serving. Drink it at the same time as a friend for a **Cheers!**, and mind the [Tipsy](/tipsy/) effect.

</Steps>

<Aside title="Don't want to wait four days?">
Find a village tavern. Its barrels are already filled with drinks to tap, one of them aged. See [Villages & taverns](/villages/).
</Aside>
