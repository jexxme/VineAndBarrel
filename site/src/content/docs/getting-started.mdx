Vine & Barrel brings a vineyard, a brewhouse and a cellar to Minecraft. You grow grapes, hops and barley, brew juice and
wort in a copper kettle, let it ferment and age in oak barrels, and share a round with your friends. Patience pays: an
aged drink's buff is stronger and lasts longer, and it shimmers in the glass.

## Install

Find your Minecraft version and your loader, and download from that cell:

<table>
	<thead><tr><th>Minecraft</th><th>NeoForge</th><th>Fabric</th></tr></thead>
	<tbody>
		{downloads.map((r) => (
			<tr>
				<td>{r.minecraft}</td>
				<td>{cell(r.neoforge)}</td>
				<td>{cell(r.fabric)}</td>
			</tr>
		))}
	</tbody>
</table>

- **NeoForge** needs {downloads.map((r) => `NeoForge ${r.neoforgeNeeds ?? `${meta.neoforge} or newer`} on Minecraft ${r.minecraft}`).join('; ')}.
- **Fabric** needs Fabric Loader {meta.fabricLoader} or newer and Fabric API {meta.fabricApi} or newer.

The file name says which one you have: <code>VineAndBarrel-{meta.minecraft}-{meta.modVersion}.jar</code> is NeoForge,
<code>VineAndBarrel-{meta.minecraft}-{meta.fabricModVersion}.jar</code> is Fabric. Put it in the `mods` folder on both the client and the server. It
needs no other mod. Everything it works with is optional and switches on by itself: see [Compatibility](/compatibility/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Playing with Farmer's Delight?">
Vine & Barrel is a mod of its own, but it fits right in: its kettle brews on FD's stove, grape and hop vines climb FD's
ropes, and a handful of cooking pot meals, a feast and a bread use its drinks. See [Farmer's Delight](/farmers-delight/).
</Aside>

## Where to start

- **New to the mod?** Follow [Your first drink](/guides/first-drink/): from a fresh world to your first glass of aged red wine, step by step.
- **Ready to brew in bulk?** [Building a cellar](/guides/cellar/) covers barrel racks, automation and planning a batch.
- **In a hurry?** Find a village tavern: its barrels are full and waiting to be tapped. See [Villages & taverns](/villages/).
- **Rather watch than read?** In game, a tooltip that ends with "Press G to watch" has a short scene: press G over the item, or while looking at the block, and someone brews it for you, step by step. G can be changed under Controls. The **Brewer's path** has the whole journey, from vine to glass, ticked off as you go: press G at anything that has no scene of its own, or click the path icon in any scene.
- **Looking something up?** Every item has its own page in [All items & blocks](/items/), every recipe is in [All recipes](/reference/recipes/), and the server and client options are in [Configuration](/reference/config/).
