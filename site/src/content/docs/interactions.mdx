Vine & Barrel tries to feel like part of the game. Almost everything below works like something you already know from
vanilla or Farmer's Delight, so you can guess the controls without reading. This page lists them all in one place; the
mechanic pages explain them in more detail.

## Aging Barrel

<Render name="scenes/barrel-cellar" float="right" size={224} alt="A wall of stacked barrels" />

- **Placed facing you**, tap toward you, like a furnace.
- **Racks:** barrels side by side with the same facing join their cradles, like fences connect.
- **Stacks:** a barrel on a barrel rests on chocks instead of a cradle. Build a cellar wall.
- **The bung shows the stage:** cork, bubbling airlock while fermenting, red wax once aged.
- **Fermenting is audible:** bubbles from the airlock, getting rarer as it finishes.
- **The tap drips** now and then while there's drink inside, in the drink's colour, like honey from a hive.
- **The aged moment:** a soft cork pop when the batch turns aged.
- **Pour in** with a bucket or bottle, like filling a cauldron. Until it's a drink, an empty bucket or bottle takes it back.
- **Tap** with an empty mug or glass, like taking a serving from a Farmer's Delight feast. The wrong vessel gets a hint and isn't used up.
- **Status line** on an empty-hand right-click. An anvil name shows up in it.
- **Comparator** reads the servings left.
- **Break it full** and it keeps its batch, like a shulker box. Aging pauses until you place it again.
- **Dispensers** tap with a vessel or pour in a base. **Hoppers** feed it and empty it.

See [Aging Barrel](/aging-barrel/).

## Brew Kettle

- **Supports, like the cooking pot:** an iron tray over a campfire, fire or lava; flush on other heat; sneak and right-click
  with an empty hand to hang it from a handle.
- **The lid lifts** while someone has the screen open, like a chest. **The sight glass** beside the tap shows how full
  it is.
- **While brewing**, steam rises from the chimney and the brew boils. **When done**, a puff of steam, and the tap drips
  now and then. A lit fire under an idle kettle sends its smoke up the chimney.
- **Place 8 kettles as a 2×2×2 cube** for a Big Brew Kettle.
- **Right-click with a bottle or bucket** to fill it, like a cauldron.
- **Break it full** and it keeps its contents.
- **Comparator** gives a signal while anything is inside. **Hoppers** feed ingredients and empty containers in, and pull the filled ones out below.
- **Copper:** it weathers over time; honeycomb waxes it, an axe scrapes it, lightning cleans it.
- **Recipe book** in its screen.

See [Brew Kettle](/brew-kettle/).

## Cauldrons

<Render name="scenes/cauldrons" float="right" size={192} alt="Cauldrons of honey must, barley wort and grape juice" />

A cauldron takes juice, wort and honey must the way it takes water.

- **Pour in** with a bucket to fill it, or with a bottle for one level. An empty bottle takes a level out, an empty
  bucket a full cauldron.
- **{rules.servingsPerBucket} levels**, one per serving, so a bucket splits into bottles and bottles make a bucket again.
- **A bucket pours over** water, lava or another kind, as vanilla's buckets do, but not over its own kind: top that up
  with bottles. Bottles only go into an empty cauldron or one of the same kind.
- **Like water**, it puts out a burning mob, a comparator reads the level, and it breaks into a plain cauldron.

## Drinks and vessels

<Render name="scenes/placed-drinks" float="right" size={192} alt="Mugs and glasses on a block" />

- **Set drinks down:** sneak and right-click the top of a block with a mug or glass, full or empty. Up to four per block,
  like candles. Right-click with an empty hand to pick one up.
- **Cheers!** Two or more players finishing a drink within {rules.cheersWindowTicks / 20} seconds and {rules.cheersRadius}
  blocks of each other clink glasses, and everyone's buff lasts {rules.cheersBonusPercent}% longer.
- **Drinking** sounds and looks like a potion, and you get the empty vessel back.
- **Buckets of juice and wort** can't be poured out as a liquid, like milk, but they fill a [cauldron](#cauldrons).

## Tipsy

- **Other players see you sway** and see your hiccups, stumbles and trips.
- **Sleep it off:** sleeping through the night clears Tipsy.
- **Milk** clears it; **water or any juice** lowers it by one level. With Farmer's Delight, so do its milk bottle,
  melon juice and apple cider.
- **Hover over the effect** in your inventory to see what your level does.
- **One drink too many** at the top level and you black out. Where you wake up is anyone's guess.

See [Tipsy](/tipsy/).

## Crops and farm life

- Bushes are picked with a right-click (half-ripe too) and drop back a stage, like sweet berries. A trellis in or above a bush lets it climb: right-click the bush with it, or the trellis with grapes or hops.
- Bees help grapes, hops and barley grow.
- Barley feeds and breeds animals like wheat; its seeds feed chickens and parrots.
- Farmer villagers plant and harvest barley, and buy barley and red grapes, as they buy wheat.
- Foxes pick grapes off the bushes, red and white, like they do sweet berries.
- Every crop composts.
- The <Item id="barley_bale" /> softens a fall and lifts campfire smoke like a hay bale.

See [Crops](/crops/).

## World

- **Village taverns** have barrels that are already full, waiting to be tapped. See [Villages & taverns](/villages/).
- **JEI** shows brewing and aging recipes, plus info pages for the barrel and the wild crops. **Jade** shows what's in a
  kettle or barrel when you look at it. See [Compatibility](/compatibility/).
