## Grape and hop bushes

Plant <Item id="red_grapes" plural /> or <Item id="hops" /> on grass, dirt or farmland, just like sweet berries. The bush
grows through {c.bushMaxAge + 1} stages at the sweet berry bush's pace.

<Stages prefix="grape_bush" title="Grape bush" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'flowering', 'ripe']} />
<Stages prefix="hop_bush" title="Hop bush" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'flowering', 'ripe']} />

- **Harvest** a ripe bush with a right-click: it gives {h.grapes.count[0]} to {h.grapes.count[1]} <Item id="red_grapes" plural /> (or
  {h.hops.count[0]} to {h.hops.count[1]} <Item id="hops" />) and drops back to its second stage, so it keeps fruiting. You
  don't need to replant.
- **Bone meal** moves it on by one stage.
- Bushes catch fire as easily as sweet berry bushes, so keep campfires and lava away from the vineyard.

<Aside title="With Farmer's Delight: vines on ropes">
Hang a Farmer's Delight rope above a ripe bush and it climbs, up to {c.ropeMaxHeight} blocks tall, and fruits along the
rope the way FD's tomatoes do. Bone meal on a ripe bush under a rope makes it climb right away. See [Farmer's Delight](/farmers-delight/).
</Aside>

## Barley

Sow <Item id="barley_seeds" /> on farmland. Barley grows exactly like wheat, through {barleyStages} stages, and
drops the same way: <Item id="barley" /> when it's ripe, plus extra seeds.

## Wild crops

<Render name="scenes/wild-crops" float="right" size={256} alt="Wild grapes, wild hops and wild barley on grass" />

Your first seeds come from wild plants. They grow in patches, by biome and temperature, so they also show up in modded
biomes that are tagged the usual way. Break one for produce, or use shears to take the whole plant home as decoration.

<WildCrops />

Farmers and the wandering trader sell seeds too, and village chests sometimes hold some. See [Villages & taverns](/villages/).

## Farm life

- **Bees** pollinate grapes, hops and barley and help them grow, as they do with vanilla crops.
- **Animals:** barley breeds and feeds {animalList('minecraft:cow_food')} like wheat does, and
  {animalList('minecraft:horse_food')} eat it. <Item id="barley_seeds" /> feed {animalList('minecraft:chicken_food')} and
  {animalList('minecraft:parrot_food')}.
- **Foxes** love grapes: they pick ripe grapes off the bushes, just like they raid sweet berry bushes.
- **Farmers** plant and harvest barley in their fields.
- **Composter:** every crop composts with vanilla's chances.

## Storage blocks

<Render name="scenes/storage" float="right" size={256} alt="Grape Crate, Hops Crate and Barley Bale" />

Pack nine of a crop into a <Item id="grape_crate" />, <Item id="hops_crate" /> or <Item id="barley_bale" />, and unpack it
again in the crafting grid. The <Item id="barley_bale" /> is a hay bale in every way: it softens a fall, a campfire on top of
it sends its smoke up high, and llamas and horses eat it.
