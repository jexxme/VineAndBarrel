## Grape and hop bushes

Plant <Item id="red_grapes" plural />, <Item id="white_grapes" plural /> or <Item id="hops" /> on grass, dirt or farmland,
just like sweet berries. The bush grows through {c.bushMaxAge + 1} stages at the sweet berry bush's pace. Red and white grapes
grow the same way; only the ripe fruit tells them apart.

<Stages prefix="grape_bush" title="Red grape bush" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'half-ripe', 'ripe']} />
<Stages prefix="white_grape_bush" title="White grape bush" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'half-ripe', 'ripe']} />
<Stages prefix="hop_bush" title="Hop bush" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'half-ripe', 'ripe']} />

- **Harvest** a ripe bush with a right-click: it gives {h.grapes.count[0]} to {h.grapes.count[1]} grapes (or
  {h.hops.count[0]} to {h.hops.count[1]} <Item id="hops" />) and drops back to its second stage, so it keeps fruiting. You
  don't need to replant.
- **In a hurry?** Pick it half-ripe, like sweet berries: {h.grapes.halfRipe[0]} to {h.grapes.halfRipe[1]} instead, and it
  drops back the same way.
- **Bone meal** moves it on by one stage.
- Bushes catch fire as easily as sweet berry bushes, so keep campfires and lava away from the vineyard.

## Trellis

<Render name="scenes/trellis" float="right" size={256} alt="Grape and hop vines on trellises, and a bare trellis" />

Put a <Item id="trellis" /> into a bush (right-click the bush with it), or plant the bush into a trellis standing on the ground. Once the bush is
ripe it climbs the trellises stacked above it, up to {c.climbMaxHeight} blocks tall, fruiting all the way up. A trellis faces you when you place it and lines up with the one below it, so rows stay straight.

- Each vine grows, fruits and gets picked like the bush, half-ripe too. Bone meal on a ripe bush starts the climb right away.
- Breaking a vine or the bush gives you its fruit and leaves the trellis standing.
- A trellis on top of a bush works too.
- A trellis isn't a ladder: everything on it is in reach from the ground.
- A row frames itself: posts where it ends, a rail along the top.

## Fences, walls, chains and bars

<Render name="scenes/supports" float="right" size={256} alt="Grape and hop vines grown into an oak fence row and a mossy wall" />

No trellis at hand? Grapes and hops also climb **fences, walls, chains and bars**, both ways round, like a trellis:
right-click one that stands on grass, dirt or farmland with grapes or hops, or right-click a bush with one. The plant then
climbs what is stacked above it, up to {c.climbMaxHeight} blocks tall.

- Each pick gives one fewer than on a trellis: {h.grapes_on_support.count[0]} to {h.grapes_on_support.count[1]} when
  ripe, {h.grapes_on_support.halfRipe[0]} when half-ripe. The trellis stays the best support.
- The block stays exactly as it was: a spruce fence stays a spruce fence, fences and walls keep connecting, a lead stays
  tied. Breaking the plant leaves it standing.
- Chains only count standing upright. Ladders, glass panes and fence gates hold nothing.
- A fence or wall has to stand in the plant: a bush with one put on top of it stays a bush. Chains and bars over a bush
  are climbed, like a trellis.
- Other mods' fences and walls work too, and packs can add blocks to the `#vineandbarrel:makeshift_vine_supports` tag.

## Wild crops

<Render name="scenes/wild-crops" float="right" size={256} alt="Wild red grapes, wild white grapes, wild hops and wild barley on grass" />

Your first seeds come from wild plants. They grow in patches, by biome and temperature, so they also show up in modded
biomes that are tagged the usual way. Break one for produce, or use shears to take the whole plant home as decoration.

<WildCrops />

Farmers and the wandering trader sell seeds too, and village chests sometimes hold some. See [Villages & taverns](/villages/).

## Farm life

- **Bees** pollinate grapes, hops and barley and help them grow, as they do with vanilla crops.
- **Animals:** <Item id="barley" /> feeds and breeds {list(eaters('vineandbarrel:barley').filter((a) => a !== 'horses' && a !== 'llamas'))} like
  wheat does. Horses and llamas heal and grow up on it just as they do on wheat, and on the <Item id="barley_bale" /> as on a hay bale.
  <Item id="barley_seeds" /> feed {list(eaters('vineandbarrel:barley_seeds'))}, and you can breed {list(eaters('vineandbarrel:red_grapes'))} with grapes.
- **Foxes** love grapes, red and white: they pick them off the bushes, just like they raid sweet berry bushes. Vines up on a
  trellis or rope are out of their reach, and they stop when mob griefing is off.
- **Farmers** plant and harvest barley in their fields and compost spare seeds. Grapes and hops they leave to you.
- **Composter:** every crop composts with vanilla's chances.

## Good to know

- **Pick, don't break.** Breaking a young bush drops nothing, and the plant is gone. Breaking one with fruit drops it (more
  with Fortune), but a right-click picks it and keeps the bush.
- **Walk right through.** Grape and hop bushes don't prick or slow you the way sweet berry bushes do.
- **Light and water:** a bush only grows in good light. Water washes bushes away, and pistons pop them.
- **Grapes are a snack,** like sweet berries.
- **More wild plants:** bone meal on a wild crop often plants another one nearby, an easy way to get your first seeds.
  Wild barley drops one barley and a seed.
- **Farmland under barley** never turns back into dirt, as with any crop.
- **Vines** need light too: in the dark they break, and the trellis or rope stays. Bone meal on a full-grown plant passes up
  to the vine above it. A vine on a rope can be climbed like the rope.
- **Bees** never make a bush climb; only you can start that.
