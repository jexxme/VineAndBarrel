## Taverns

Every village type, from plains to snowy, can have a **tavern** built in its own style. You'll find one in roughly every
second village, and now and then two.

Its barrels are **already full**: walk in, grab a mug or glass, and tap a drink. One of them is even aged. The tavern chest holds seeds, mugs and glasses, and a few drinks of the region,
everything you need to start brewing yourself.

<Shot src="terrace.jpg" caption="A tavern terrace: tables of drinks under the pergolas, the barrel cellar against the wall." />

## Village farms

Village farms grow our crops alongside the vanilla ones, depending on the climate: grapes where it's warm, hops where it's cool,
barley nearly everywhere.
