## Taverns

Every village type can have a **tavern**, built in its own style, in one of two layouts. You'll find one in about one
village in three, never more than one per village. No two are quite alike: what's in the casks, the drinks on the
tables, the flowers, banners and candles change from tavern to tavern.

Look for the hanging sign over the door. Inside there's room to move: a table by the fire, stools at the bar, the kettle
on its campfire behind it and the [tavern keeper](#tavern-keeper) by the kettle. Behind the bar, stairs lead down to the
**cellar**.

Each village type has a feature of its own:

- **Plains:** a beer garden, long tables under a pergola of hop bines.
- **Desert:** a roof terrace, up the outside stairs.
- **Savanna:** a veranda bar in the shade, with a counter of its own.
- **Snowy:** a hearth room with a big fireplace and fur rugs.
- **Taiga:** a mead hall with a long table, and a woodpile under the eaves.

And each has a cellar of its own:

- **Plains:** the beer cellar, a store of malt and hops, with a trapdoor down to the old vault.
- **Desert:** the wine cellar, with a cistern vault below and a chest behind a pot in a niche.
- **Savanna:** the press cellar, a treading room with a pit of grape crates, an old cask and a chest under the stairs.
- **Snowy:** the ice cellar, casks in the snow and an ice well down to the cold store, with a chest buried in the snow.
- **Taiga:** the root cellar, with the old store behind a curtain of hanging roots.

Its barrels are **already full**: walk in, grab a mug or glass, and tap a drink. A couple of them are aged. The chest by
the bar holds what a house chest of the village holds, plus some seeds, mugs and glasses and a drink of the region. A chest
hidden in the cellar holds seeds and a drink, more often an aged one.

### Good to know

- Tavern barrels are already past fermenting, so they can't be topped up. Tap one empty and fill it with your own brew:
  an aged drink from a barrel you filled yourself is what earns *Patience Pays*.
- The young tavern barrels keep aging like any barrel, so a tavern you leave alone for a few days ends up with more aged drinks.
- Tapping any barrel in a village, a tavern's or your own, earns *On Tap*.
- Do your trading sober: from Tipsy {roman(rules.tipsy.cutOffLevel - 1)}, villagers and traders won't deal with you. See [Tipsy](/tipsy/#rowdy).

## Tavern keeper

Every tavern has its **tavern keeper**, behind the bar by the kettle, in a wine-red apron with a striped bar towel. He's a
villager like any other: he works, trades, levels up and sleeps.

- **His job site** is the <Item id="brew_kettle" />, at every copper stage, waxed or not. A Big Brew Kettle is one job
  site, for one keeper. A villager without a job may claim a kettle of yours too, the way a farmer claims a composter.
- **What he trades:** at first he buys barley and hops and sells mugs and wine glasses. Then he buys grapes and sells
  <Item id="barley_ale" /> and <Item id="wheat_beer" />, then his region's wines, mead and cider (and buys honey, berries,
  apples or melon for them), then aged drinks.
- **His region's drink:** <Item id="barley_ale" /> in the plains, <Item id="red_wine" /> in the desert,
  <Item id="melon_wine" /> in the savanna and jungle, <Item id="mead" /> in snowy villages, <Item id="sweet_berry_wine" />
  in the taiga and <Item id="hard_cider" /> in the swamp.
- **The master's sealed cask:** a master keeper sells an <Item id="aging_barrel" /> full of his region's drink, aged. Place
  it and it's full and sealed, ready to tap.
- **Cheers:** finish a drink alone in front of him and he raises his mug and clinks with you: you get the
  [Cheers!](/drinks/#cheers) bonus. He thinks a little better of you each time, so regulars pay a little less.
- **Restocking:** once a day he fills an empty cask of his own tavern, one he can walk to, with a couple of servings of
  his region's young drink. He never touches your own casks, and what he pours never counts as your own brew.

## Village farms

Village farms grow our crops alongside the vanilla ones, depending on the climate: red grapes where it's warm, white grapes
in plains and taiga villages, hops where it's cool, barley nearly everywhere.

<VillageInfo show="farms" />

## Trades

Farmers and the wandering trader sell seeds, hops and grapes, and farmers buy barley and red grapes, as they buy wheat. The
tavern keeper buys our crops too and sells drinks: see [Tavern keeper](#tavern-keeper).
