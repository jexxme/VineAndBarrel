export const juice = recipe('brewing/grape_juice');
export const wine = recipe('aging/red_wine');
export const young = drinks.find((d) => d.id === 'vineandbarrel:red_wine');
export const aged = drinks.find((d) => d.id === 'vineandbarrel:aged_red_wine');
export const h = rules.harvest;
export const grapesPerBatch = juice.ingredients.filter((g) => g.items.includes('vineandbarrel:red_grapes')).length;

<Render name="scenes/tavern" float="right" size={230} alt="A tavern corner with barrels and drinks on a table" />

This guide takes you from a fresh world to your first glass of red wine, then on to an aged one. You need only vanilla
materials to start; the grapes come in along the way. Each step links to the page with all the details.

<Steps>

1. **Find wild grapes.** Wild plants grow in patches out in the world, each in its own kind of place. For wine you want
   <Item id="wild_grapes" />: break one by hand for <Item id="red_grapes" plural />.

   <WildCrops />

   Can't find any? Village farmers and the wandering trader sell grapes, and village chests often hold some. See
   [Villages & taverns](/villages/).

2. **Plant a few bushes.** Plant the grapes on grass, dirt or farmland, like sweet berries. A bush grows on its own;
   once it's ripe, right-click it to pick {h.grapes.count[0]} to {h.grapes.count[1]} grapes. It drops back a stage and
   fruits again, so you never need to replant. Bone meal speeds it up.

   <Stages prefix="grape_bush" title="A grape bush growing" labels={['planted', 'growing', 'half-ripe', 'ripe']} />

   One batch of juice takes {grapesPerBatch} grapes and a sugar, so a handful of bushes is plenty to start. More on
   [Crops](/crops/).

3. **Build a Brew Kettle and put it over a fire.** The <Item id="brew_kettle" /> is made of copper. It brews only while
   the block right below it is hot: a lit campfire works from day one. It stands on an iron tray above the flames.

   <div class="not-content" style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:30px 40px"><Recipe id="brew_kettle" /></div>

4. **Brew grape juice.** Right-click the kettle to open it and put in {grapesPerBatch} grapes and a sugar. The arrow
   fills while the kettle is hot, steam curls out from under the lid, and after {seconds(juice.brewingTime)} the gauge
   shows {juice.servings} servings of <Item id="grape_juice" />. If you put in more, it keeps brewing batches. See
   [Brew Kettle](/brew-kettle/).

   <div class="not-content"><Recipe id="brewing/grape_juice" /></div>

5. **Take it out.** Right-click the kettle with a glass bottle for one serving, or with a bucket for {rules.servingsPerBucket}.
   Grape juice is already a drink of its own: it takes the edge off [Tipsy](/tipsy/).

6. **Fill an Aging Barrel.** Place an <Item id="aging_barrel" /> and right-click it with the bucket or the bottles. It
   holds {rules.barrelCapacity} servings: two buckets fill it to the brim. Topping it up with more of the same juice
   while it ferments is fine.

   <div class="not-content"><Recipe id="aging_barrel" /></div>

7. **Wait for it to ferment.** After **{days(wine.fermentingTime)}** the juice has become <Item id="red_wine" />. The barrel
   tells you how it's doing: the glass airlock on top bubbles while it ferments, and a right-click with an empty hand
   shows a status line such as *Red Wine · 8 servings · fermenting, ready tomorrow morning*. The clock runs on game
   time, so sleeping through the night counts. See [Aging Barrel](/aging-barrel/).

8. **Tap a glass.** Right-click the barrel with an empty <Item id="wine_glass" /> to tap one serving. Wine goes in a glass,
   beer, mead and cider in a <Item id="mug" />; the barrel tells you if you hold the wrong one.

   <div class="not-content"><Recipe id="wine_glass" /></div>

9. **Drink it.** <Item id="red_wine" /> gives you <EffectBadge id={young.effect.id} name={young.effect.name} ticks={young.effect.duration} amplifier={young.effect.amplifier} /> and one level of
   <EffectBadge id="vineandbarrel:tipsy" name="Tipsy" />. You keep the glass. Drink at the same moment as a friend nearby
   and you get a **Cheers!**: everyone's buff lasts {rules.cheersBonusPercent}% longer. Finish it in front of a tavern keeper
   and he clinks with you.

10. **Let the rest age.** Leave the barrel alone for **{days(wine.agingTime)}** more and what's left becomes
    <Item id="aged_red_wine" />: a deeper red that shimmers, and <EffectBadge id={aged.effect.id} name={aged.effect.name} ticks={aged.effect.duration} amplifier={aged.effect.amplifier} />.
    A soft cork pop and a red wax seal on top tell you it's done.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Can't wait?">
Every glass you tap during the aging stage is one less that ages. Fill two barrels: drink from one, and leave the other
alone until it's aged. Or find a village tavern, whose barrels are already full, a couple of them aged. See
[Villages & taverns](/villages/).
</Aside>

## Where to go next

- Brew the other drinks: white grapes for white wine, sweet berries for sweet berry wine, barley and hops for pale ale,
  wheat and hops for wheat beer, honey for mead, apples for hard cider. See
  [Drinks](/drinks/) and [Crops](/crops/).
- Put a few barrels side by side and build a proper cellar: [Building a cellar](/guides/cellar/).
- Mind your limits: what each level of [Tipsy](/tipsy/) does, and how to sober up.
- With Farmer's Delight, brew over its stove and cook with your wine: [Farmer's Delight](/farmers-delight/).
