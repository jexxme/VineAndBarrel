Vine & Barrel {meta.modVersion} runs on **Minecraft {meta.minecraft}** with **NeoForge {meta.neoforge}** or newer. It needs no
other mods. Everything below is optional and switches on by itself when the other mod is installed.

## Farmer's Delight

Stove heat, vines on ropes, rich soil, drinks from FD's bottles and two cooking pot meals. See [Farmer's Delight](/farmers-delight/).

## JEI

JEI shows a **Brew Kettle** and an **Aging Barrel** recipe category, with brewing times and the days each stage takes,
plus info pages for the barrel and for the wild crops. Built against JEI {meta.jei}.

## Jade

Look at a kettle or barrel to see what's inside: the brew or drink, the servings left, how far the brew has got, whether the
kettle is heated, and what stage the barrel is at. Built against Jade {meta.jade}.

## Biomes O' Plenty

Wild crops grow in every biome tagged as a forest, plains, savanna or river (where the temperature suits them), so modded biomes with those tags get them automatically.
With Biomes O' Plenty they also grow in a few biomes that suit them:

<ul>
	{bop.map((b) => <li><Item id={b.crop} />: {list(b.biomes)}</li>)}
</ul>

## Serene Seasons

With Serene Seasons, each crop grows in its own seasons:

<ul>
	{['vineandbarrel:red_grapes', 'vineandbarrel:hops', 'vineandbarrel:barley_seeds'].map((id) => (
		<li><Item id={id} />: {list(seasonRows.filter(([, ids]) => ids.includes(id)).map(([s]) => s))}</li>
	))}
</ul>
