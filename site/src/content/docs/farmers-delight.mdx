Vine & Barrel is a standalone mod: everything on this wiki works without Farmer's Delight. If you play with Farmer's Delight
too, the two work together in a few places. Nothing needs setting up.

On Minecraft 26.x for NeoForge, that's Farmer's Delight Neoforged, version {fd.minVersion} or newer, which has its own wiki.
On Fabric, it's <a href={fd.refabricated}>Farmer's Delight Refabricated</a>.

<LinkCard title="Farmer's Delight Neoforged wiki" description="Cooking pot, stove, ropes, rich soil and every FD item." href={`${FDN_WIKI}/`} />

## What Farmer's Delight adds

- **The stove heats the kettle.** Farmer's Delight's stove works as a heat source for the [Brew Kettle](/brew-kettle/),
  and so does anything else FD counts as heat. Blocks FD lets heat pass through pass it up to the kettle too.
- **Vines climb ropes.** A ripe grape bush (red or white) or hop bush with an FD rope above it climbs up to {rules.crops.climbMaxHeight} blocks
  and fruits along the rope, like FD's tomatoes, just as it does on a [trellis](/crops/#trellis). Ropes and trellises mix in one
  plant, and breaking a vine leaves the rope.
- **Rich soil** speeds up grapes, hops and barley, as it does every crop you can use bone meal on.
- **FD's drinks ferment.** <Item id="farmersdelight:apple_cider" /> and <Item id="farmersdelight:melon_juice" /> bottles go into an
  [Aging Barrel](/aging-barrel/) like our own bases, one serving each. FD's apple cider becomes the same
  <Item id="hard_cider" /> that our <Item id="apple_juice" /> makes, so there is one cider, two ways in. A barrel holds one
  base at a time, so the two don't mix in one batch. FD's bottles come only as bottles, so a full barrel takes
  {rules.barrelCapacity} of them; dispensers pour them in too.
- **Cooking pot meals, a feast and a bread** use our wine, cider and beer.
- **FD's knife cuts straw from barley**, as it does from wheat and wild rice.
- Our drinks count as FD drinks wherever FD asks for one.

## Coq au Vin

A feast, like Farmer's Delight's: cook it in the pot without a bowl, and it comes out as a pot of <Item id="coq_au_vin_block" />
to set down on a table. Use a bowl on it to serve a <Item id="coq_au_vin" />, four in all. After the last one, use it once
more to clear the bones. It shows up in the recipe book once you carry red wine.

## Straw from barley

Harvest ripe barley with a Farmer's Delight knife and you get a <Item id="farmersdelight:straw" /> too, as from wheat.
On the cutting board, a knife cuts <Item id="wild_barley" /> into barley, and half the time a straw, like FD's wild rice.

## Without Farmer's Delight

The meals, the feast, the bread and melon wine still exist, so worlds and inventories stay intact, but you can't make them, the creative tab
and JEI hide them, and the meals give no Nourishment. Hard cider is still made from our apple juice. Took Farmer's Delight out with hard cider still in a barrel? The batch finishes as usual; you
just can't take the base back out any more.
