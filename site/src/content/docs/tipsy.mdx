Every wine, beer and cider gives you its buff **and one level of <EffectBadge id="vineandbarrel:tipsy" name="Tipsy" />**. It's
fun at first: a sway, hiccups, and a little liquid courage. Keep going and it starts to cost you: dark edges, heavy eyes,
queasy spells, weak arms, then heavy legs and stumbling. It tops out at level {t.maxLevel}.

## Levels

<TipsyLevels />

Other players see you sway a little and see your hiccups too. Hover over the Tipsy effect in your inventory to see what
your current level does.

## One drink too many

Already at level {t.maxLevel} and still drinking? You **black out**. Your eyes fall shut, and for a few seconds nothing can
hurt you. Then you wake up somewhere else, usually at your bed. You'll be hungry and shaky for a while, back at Tipsy I,
and your pockets may hold something you don't remember picking up.

## Sobering up

- **Wait:** each level wears off after **{cfg.secondsPerLevel} seconds**, one level at a time. A new drink resets the clock
  of your current level.
- **Milk** clears it completely, like any effect.
- **A water bottle or <Item id="grape_juice" />** lowers it by one level.
- **Sleep it off:** going to bed and sleeping through the night clears it.

## Settings

Server owners can change Tipsy in `vineandbarrel-tipsy-server.toml`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | <code>{String(cfg.enabled)}</code> | `false` is the non-alcoholic mode: drinks never make you tipsy, but keep their buffs and names. |
| `maxLevel` | <code>{cfg.maxLevel}</code> | The highest level drinks can stack to. |
| `secondsPerLevel` | <code>{cfg.secondsPerLevel}</code> | How long each level lasts. |
| `slurredChat` | <code>{String(cfg.slurredChat)}</code> | Slurred chat from level {t.slurLevel}. |
| `stumbling` | <code>{String(cfg.stumbling)}</code> | Stumbling at level {t.stumbleLevel}. |
| `blackout` | <code>{String(cfg.blackout)}</code> | Black out after one drink too many at the top level. |

And every player can tune the visuals in `vineandbarrel-tipsy-client.toml`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `wobbleIntensity` | <code>{cfg.wobbleIntensity}</code> | Camera sway strength, from 0 (off) to 2. Set it to 0 if motion makes you queasy; it always wins over the server. |
| `otherPlayersSway` | <code>{String(cfg.otherPlayersSway)}</code> | Show tipsy players swaying. |
| `visionEffects` | <code>{String(cfg.visionEffects)}</code> | The dark screen edges and the heavy-eyed blinks. |
