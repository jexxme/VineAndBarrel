Every wine, beer, cider and mead gives you its buff **and one level of <EffectBadge id="vineandbarrel:tipsy" name="Tipsy" />**. It's
fun at first: a sway, hiccups, a little liquid courage, and at Tipsy {roman(t.slurLevel - 1)} you're at your best (and
your chat isn't), though your eyes start to get heavy. Keep going and it starts to cost you: queasy spells, weak arms,
and at the top, legs that barely carry you home. It tops out at level {t.maxLevel}.

## Levels

<TipsyLevels />

Other players see you sway, hiccup, stumble and trip too. Hover over the Tipsy effect in your inventory to see what
your current level does.

## One drink too many

Already at level {t.maxLevel} and still drinking? You **black out**. Your eyes fall shut and you keel over, stars
circling your head for everyone to see. For those few seconds nobody can hurt you, no mob and no player, and keeling
over doesn't either; the rest of the world still counts, so don't pass out in lava. Then you wake up. Where? That
depends on the night, and there are more ways to wake up than you'd think…

You'll be hungry and shaky for a while, back at Tipsy I, and your pockets may hold something you don't remember picking
up. Nothing you had is ever lost.

## Rowdy

From Tipsy {roman(rowdyLevel - 1)}, you get rowdy. Now and then your arm swings by itself and throws a real punch at
whatever is right in front of you: {punchHearts === 0.5 ? 'half a heart' : `${punchHearts} hearts`} of damage
and a shove, whatever you're holding. It never hits tamed pets, iron golems or armor stands, and it only hits other players
where PvP is on. Villagers and passers-by are fair game, though, and they remember it.

You're also **cut off**: from Tipsy {roman(t.cutOffLevel - 1)}, villagers and wandering traders shake their heads and won't
trade with you until you've sobered up. Do your trading before the {t.cutOffLevel === 4 ? 'fourth' : `${t.cutOffLevel}th`} drink.

## Sobering up

- **Wait:** each level wears off after **{cfg.secondsPerLevel} seconds**, one level at a time. A new drink gives every level
  a fresh clock, so at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)} you're sober again after about {Math.round((t.maxLevel * cfg.secondsPerLevel) / 6) / 10} minutes
  without another drop.
- **Milk** clears it completely, like any effect. It clears your drink buffs too.
- **A water bottle or any juice** (<Item id="grape_juice" />, <Item id="white_grape_juice" />, <Item id="sweet_berry_juice" />,
  <Item id="apple_juice" />) lowers it by one level right away. Only a plain water bottle works, no other potion.
- **With Farmer's Delight**, its <Item id="farmersdelight:milk_bottle" />, <Item id="farmersdelight:melon_juice" /> and
  <Item id="farmersdelight:apple_cider" /> lower it by one level too. A milk bottle takes one level; a bucket of milk
  clears it all.
- **Sleep it off:** go to bed and sleep through the night. It has to be a night that's really skipped: get up early, or lie
  awake while the others on the server stay up, and you wake up as tipsy as you went to bed.

## Good to know

- **Sneak** at Tipsy {roman(t.stumbleLevel - 1)} and your legs hold: no drift, no stumbles, no trips. Swimming, riding and
  flying are safe too.
- **A trip never hurts** and never puts you anywhere you didn't walk. You only go down on flat, open ground with no
  monster near, and not twice within {Math.round(t.tripCooldownTicks / 20)} seconds; anywhere else it stays a stumble.
- **Water gets you out of the bad levels fast,** but the levels below keep their own time, so it doesn't make you sober
  sooner overall.
- Tipsy only looks after its own side effects: a Slowness or Nausea from a potion or a command is left alone.
- Chat links, commands and @mentions are never slurred.
- Other players see you sway, hear your hiccups and see your stumbles, trips and rowdy swings, but not your dark screen
  edges or your blinks.
- **Want the whole story of a blackout?** Every place you can wake up, the odds and the souvenirs are on
  [Blackouts](/blackouts/). Spoilers, of course.

## Settings

Server owners can switch Tipsy off entirely (the non-alcoholic mode) or turn off slurred chat, stumbling (with the trips
and the drift), blackouts, Rowdy's punches and the cut-off one by one. Every player can tone down how much Tipsy moves
their screen, down to not at all, and switch off the vision effects; vanilla's Distortion Effects, FOV Effects and Darkness Pulsing
sliders count too. All options and defaults are on [Configuration](/reference/config/).
