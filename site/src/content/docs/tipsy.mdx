Every wine, beer, cider and mead gives you its buff **and one level of <EffectBadge id="vineandbarrel:tipsy" name="Tipsy" />**.
Being drunk shows in your body, not on your screen: your legs get a mind of their own, your eyes see double, the world
goes woolly. It's fun at first: wobbly legs, hiccups, a little liquid courage, and at Tipsy {roman(t.slurLevel - 1)} you're at
your best (and your chat isn't). Keep going and it starts to cost you: no more buffs, picking fights, nodding off on your
feet, and at the top, legs that barely carry you home. It tops out at level {t.maxLevel}.

## Drunk legs

From Tipsy {roman(t.drunkWalkLevel - 1)}, your legs have a mind of their own. What you steer arrives a moment late, so you
get going late and can't stop on the spot. The walk curves off course and back, and you keep correcting. Your body rocks
onto each foot and the steps come uneven. Your own view only leans a little with your body; other players see the whole
wobble, and the path you really take.

From Tipsy {roman(t.stumbleLevel - 1)} you **stumble**: a misstep to the side, arms out for balance. Once in a while one isn't
caught and you **trip**, flat on your face, and get back up a moment later. Your legs get heavy too: you're
{pct(slower(t.slowerLevel))} slower at Tipsy {roman(t.slowerLevel - 1)} and {pct(slower(t.maxLevel))} slower at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)}.
You can still sprint.

The drunk walk never takes you over an edge or into anything that hurts. **Sneak** and you walk straight. Swimming,
riding, flying and climbing stay sober.

## Eyes and ears

From Tipsy {roman(t.softEdgesLevel - 1)}, the edges of your screen go soft and a little warm. From Tipsy {roman(t.doubleVisionLevel - 1)}
you **see double**: a second image slides apart and back, further with every level. **Hold sneak to squint** and the two
come closer together, though it helps less the drunker you are. The writing on signs fades into the wood from
Tipsy {roman(t.signBlurLevel - 1)}, until at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)} you can barely read it; squinting helps there too.

From Tipsy {roman(t.woollyLevel - 1)} the world sounds **woolly**, muffled as if through a blanket, more with every level.
Music and menus stay clear. At Tipsy {roman(t.heartbeatLevel - 1)} you hear your own heart beating, and only you hear it.

## Nodding off

From Tipsy {roman(t.nodLevel - 1)}, don't stand around too long. Stay still a while and your lids sink, your head drops,
and then you jerk awake with a snort. Stand on and it comes back. Anything you do wakes you: a step, a look around, using
something. Everyone around you sees your chin sink and hears the snort.

## Drunk chat

Your chat comes out the way you'd say it. At Tipsy {roman(t.hicChatLevel - 1)} the odd \*hic\* slips in, at Tipsy {roman(chatAt(2) - 1)}
it's slurred but easy to read, at Tipsy {roman(chatAt(3) - 1)} it gets hard to read, and at Tipsy {roman(chatAt(4) - 1)} it's
gibberish. Links, @mentions and commands always go out as you typed them.

## Rowdy

From Tipsy {roman(rowdyLevel - 1)}, you get rowdy. Now and then you're spoiling for a fight, for up to
{Math.round(t.rowdyItchTicks / 20)} seconds, and the first one in front of you gets it, even if you only glance their way.
Your arm draws back for a moment, so everyone sees it coming, then you throw a real punch:
{punchHearts === 0.5 ? 'half a heart' : `${punchHearts} hearts`} of damage and a shove, whatever you're holding. The swing
carries you a step forward; if they got out of the way, you miss and stumble. It never hits tamed pets, iron golems or
armor stands, and it only hits other players where PvP is on. Villagers and passers-by are fair game, though, and they
remember it.

You're also **cut off**: from Tipsy {roman(t.cutOffLevel - 1)}, villagers and wandering traders shake their heads and won't
trade with you until you've sobered up. Do your trading before the {t.cutOffLevel === 4 ? 'fourth' : `${t.cutOffLevel}th`} drink.

## One drink too many

Already at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)} and still drinking? That drink doesn't knock you out on the spot. It **catches up
with you** {Math.round(under.soonestTicks / 20)} to {Math.round(under.latestTicks / 20)} seconds later, whatever you're doing
by then. For the last {Math.round(under.ticks / 20)} seconds you feel yourself going under: your lids get heavy, your head
sinks, the world goes muffled and far away, your legs give and you sway hard. Your friends see it coming too.

Then you **black out**. Your eyes fall shut and you keel over, stars circling your head for everyone to see. For those few
seconds nobody can hurt you, no mob and no player, and keeling over doesn't either; the rest of the world still counts, so
don't pass out in lava. Then you wake up. Where? That depends on the night, and there are more ways to wake up than you'd
think…

You wake up sober but [hungover](#the-morning-after), and your pockets may hold something you don't remember picking up.
Nothing you had is ever lost. Sobering up before it hits (a bucket of milk, a night's sleep) gets you out of it; another
drink never puts it off.

## The morning after

After a blackout, or a night's sleep at Tipsy {roman(h.sleepLevel - 1)} or higher, you wake up sober but with a
<EffectBadge id="vineandbarrel:hangover" name="Hangover" />. You're hungry, too: no more than {h.hungryFood / 2} hunger
shanks, and nothing in your saturation.

- **Daylight glares.** Under the open sky the bright day hurts and you squint into it. Right after waking it gets you even
  indoors, easing off over about {Math.round(h.freshGlareTicks / 20)} seconds. Holding sneak to squint helps.
- **Loud noises** close by make you wince.
- Nothing moves your view and nothing makes you sick.

It lasts {duration(h.afterBlackoutTicks)} after a blackout, {duration(h.afterSleepTicks[h.sleepLevel])} after sleeping at
Tipsy {roman(h.sleepLevel - 1)} and {duration(h.afterSleepTicks[t.maxLevel])} at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)}. **A water bottle or any juice** takes
{pct(1 - h.drinkLeaves)} of what's left away.

## Sobering up

- **Wait:** each level wears off after **{cfg.secondsPerLevel} seconds**, one level at a time. A new drink gives every level
  a fresh clock, so at Tipsy {roman(t.maxLevel - 1)} you're sober again after about {Math.round((t.maxLevel * cfg.secondsPerLevel) / 6) / 10} minutes
  without another drop.
- **Milk** clears it completely, like any effect. It clears your drink buffs too.
- **A water bottle or any juice** (<Item id="grape_juice" />, <Item id="white_grape_juice" />, <Item id="sweet_berry_juice" />,
  <Item id="apple_juice" />) lowers it by one level right away. Only a plain water bottle works, no other potion.
- **With Farmer's Delight**, its <Item id="farmersdelight:milk_bottle" />, <Item id="farmersdelight:melon_juice" /> and
  <Item id="farmersdelight:apple_cider" /> lower it by one level too. A milk bottle takes one level; a bucket of milk
  clears it all.
- **Sleep it off:** go to bed and sleep through the night. You wake up sober, though from Tipsy {roman(h.sleepLevel - 1)} with
  a [hangover](#the-morning-after). It has to be a night that's really skipped: get up early, or lie awake while the others
  on the server stay up, and you wake up as tipsy as you went to bed.

## Good to know

- **A trip never hurts** and never puts you anywhere you didn't walk. You only go down on flat, open ground with no
  monster near, and not twice within {Math.round(t.tripCooldownTicks / 20)} seconds; anywhere else it stays a stumble.
- **Water gets you out of the bad levels fast,** but the levels below keep their own time, so it doesn't make you sober
  sooner overall.
- Other players see you wobble, stumble, trip and nod off, see your rowdy swings coming and hear your hiccups.
- **Want the whole story of a blackout?** Every place you can wake up, the odds and the souvenirs are on
  [Blackouts](/blackouts/). Spoilers, of course.

## Settings

Server owners can switch Tipsy off entirely (the non-alcoholic mode) or turn off slurred chat, drunk legs (with the
stumbles and trips), nodding off, the hangover, blackouts, Rowdy's punches and the cut-off one by one. Tipsy never moves
your screen on its own; every player can tone down how much a hiccup, a stumble or a trip moves it, down to not at all,
and vanilla's Distortion Effects slider counts too. Each player can also switch off the drunk eyes (soft edges, double
vision, fading signs and the hangover's glare) and the woolly sound. All options and defaults are on
[Configuration](/reference/config/).
